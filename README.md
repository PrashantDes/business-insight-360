# 📊 Business Insight 360 — Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

**Business Insight 360** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard designed to provide a 360° view of business performance across **Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive** functions.

The dashboard brings financial, sales, market, and supply-chain metrics into a single interactive reporting experience, allowing users to move from high-level executive KPIs to detailed product, customer, region, and forecast analysis.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to help business stakeholders:

- Monitor overall business performance
- Track revenue, gross margin, and profitability
- Compare actual performance against benchmarks and previous periods
- Analyze product and customer performance
- Understand regional and market-level performance
- Evaluate sales and market-share contribution
- Monitor supply-chain forecast accuracy and errors
- Identify areas requiring further investigation

## 🖥️ Dashboard Views

### 1. Finance View

Provides a detailed **Profit & Loss Statement** and financial performance analysis.

Key metrics include:
- Net Sales
- Gross Sales
- Net Invoice Sales
- Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
- Gross Margin
- Gross Margin %
- Operational Expense
- Net Profit
- Net Profit %


### 2. Sales View

Focuses on product and market performance, including Net Sales, Gross Margin, product performance, customer performance, and regional analysis.


### 3. Marketing View

Analyzes market and segment performance using Net Sales, Gross Margin %, Net Profit %, revenue contribution, and regional comparisons.


### 4. Supply Chain View

Focuses on forecasting and supply-chain performance.

Key metrics include:
- Forecast Accuracy %
- Net Error
- Absolute Error
- Forecast Accuracy vs previous year
- Customer-level forecast metrics
- Product/segment-level forecast metrics
- Risk classification


### 5. Executive View

Consolidates important business KPIs into a high-level management dashboard.

The displayed KPIs include:
- Net Sales
- Gross Margin %
- Net Profit %
- Forecast Accuracy %
- Revenue contribution
- Top customers and products
- Regional/sub-region performance

## 🧮 Business Metrics

The dashboard uses metrics and abbreviations including:

- **NS** — Net Sales
- **GM** — Gross Margin
- **NP** — Net Profit
- **BM** — Benchmark
- **LY** — Last Year
- **RC** — Revenue Contribution
- **MS** — Market Share
- **FCA** — Forecast Accuracy
- **Chg** — Change

All monetary values in the dashboard are presented in **millions of dollars unless otherwise indicated**.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download **`business insights.pbix`** from this repository.
2. Open it using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use the navigation tabs to explore Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive views.
4. Use the filters and slicers to perform your own analysis.

> A Power BI Desktop installation is required to interact with the `.pbix` report.
